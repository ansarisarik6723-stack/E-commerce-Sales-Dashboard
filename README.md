# E-commerce-Sales Performance Dashboard
   Power BI dashboard providing insights into e-commerce sales performance, customer behavior, payment modes, and profitability.

# Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze **e-commerce sales performance** and provide insights into customer behavior, product categories, payment modes, and profitability.  
The dashboard helps business stakeholders track revenue, profit, and sales trends across states, customers, and categories to improve decision-making.


# Key Questions (KPIs)
This dashboard answers the following business questions:
- What is the **total sales amount, quantity sold, and overall profit**?
- Which **customers** contribute the most to revenue?
- Which **payment modes** are most frequently used?
- How does **profit vary across months**?
- Which **states and categories** drive the highest sales?
- What are the **top-performing product categories**?


# Process
1. **Data Preparation**: Sales dataset collected and cleaned in Excel.  
2. **Data Modeling**: Built relationships and created calculated measures using **DAX**.  
3. **Visualization**: Designed interactive visuals (bar charts, donut charts, line charts) in **Power BI**.  
4. **Dashboard Development**: Combined KPIs and visuals into a single interactive dashboard.

# Project Insights
- **Revenue**: Total sales amount of ₹438K with overall profit of ₹36.9K.  
- **Customer Contribution**: Top customers include Harivansh, Madhav, and Madan Mohan.  
- **Payment Modes**: COD and UPI dominate transactions, while credit/debit cards are less frequent.  
- **Profit Trends**: Monthly profit fluctuates, with some months showing losses.  
- **State Analysis**: Maharashtra leads in sales, followed by Madhya Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh.  
- **Category Insights**: Clothing contributes the highest quantity sold, while Electronics and Furniture follow.  
- **Top Categories**: Printers, Bookcases, Sarees, Accessories, and Tables are the top-selling items.  


# Conclusion
The E-Commerce Sales Dashboard highlights that **sales are concentrated in specific states and categories**, with **COD and UPI** being the preferred payment modes.  
Key recommendations include:
- Focusing on **high-value customers** for loyalty programs.  
- Promoting **profitable categories** like Electronics and Furniture.  
- Addressing **loss-making months** with targeted marketing campaigns.  
- Expanding in states with lower sales to balance regional performance.  

This project demonstrates the use of **Power BI** for turning raw e-commerce data into actionable business insights.
